Dark Fantasy Soulslike SFX Pack
by Gomo Sounds - https://gomosounds.com

164 sound effects for a dark fantasy action game: a lone armored knight, swords and bows,
sorcery, hollow soldiers, plague hounds, a giant knight, an ancient dragon, bonfires, fog
gates, ambience loops, interface sounds and 4 music loops.

Format: WAV, 16-bit, 44.1 kHz. Effects are mono; music, ambience and some interface sounds
are stereo.
Almost every event comes in 3 variations (_01, _02, _03). Pick one at random each time the
event plays, so it never sounds repetitive.
Files with "_loop" in the name are made to play on repeat (ambience, music, the bonfire,
the war drum and footsteps).

Folders:
  Player/     the knight: footsteps, dodge roll, light and heavy attacks, parry, shield
              block, hurt, death, drinking a flask
  Weapons/    longsword hits (flesh and armor), longbow draw/shoot/impact, critical blow
  Sorcery/    fire sorcery (cast, explosion), dark sorcery, healing miracle
  Enemies/    hollow soldier, plague hound, giant knight, ancient dragon
  World/      bonfire (kindle, crackle loop), fog gate, cathedral door, treasure chest,
              war drum
  Ambience/   wind, rain, ruined cathedral, cursed swamp, catacombs, distant battlefield
  Interface/  click, error, open menu, pick up item, "you died", "enemy felled", level up
  Music/      main theme, bonfire rest, boss battle, lament

sound_list.csv lists every file with its category, loop flag, length and the text prompt
it was generated from.
