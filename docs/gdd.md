# Description

Description: A platformer game set in a magical library.



# Core Gameplay Loop

The Gameplay Loop: The player sorts books across the different halls of the library. Each book is a platforming level the player explores the area of the book, solve puzzles, and collect items and different relics while exploring. After a number of books, the players fights a floor boss and makes use of their collection of items to unlock more halls and progress forward until he opens the library.



Primary Mechanics: Exploration and puzzle solving. Metroidvania platforming, items collection and progress in separate levels.



Secondary Mechanics: Moving between multiple rooms "library halls" and progressive unlock of more rooms and floors. A boss fight after a set number of levels are complete.



Tertiary Mechanics: Card Deck building for a card boss fight. Skill tree, progression buffs and obtainable skills of sort



# MDA Framework

Mechanics: Linear progression but with player freedom to decide what order to progress in. Hidden rooms. Optional puzzles that might yield secret items or traps instead. Fake items.



Dynamics: PLayers explore different rooms and levels. Solve puzzles and collect items. Track progression and their collection completion. Hoarding of exploration items. Replaying levels to find missing items or spending more time to find secret rooms and puzzles in a level.



Aesthetics: A sense of freedom when picking levels or just roaming the main library. Discovery and exploration during different levels being like an adventure.



# Player Experience

How should they feel? (Incorporate Leblanc’s Taxonomy of pleasures): Fantasy of being a magical librarian. Discovery and Immersion in the different levels to explore. Feel challenged when fighting a floor boss.



Game Inspirations: Castlevania Symphony of the night, Sonic generations, Yugioh Master Duel, The Talos Principle, Library of Ruina



Non-Game Inspirations: A library that has books named after everyone that has died, and opening someone's book makes you experience their life from ReZero



Genre: Metroidvania, Platformer, Puzzle, Deck Builder



Target Audience (Incorporate Bartle’s Taxonomy): Explorers and Achievers



Progression Over Time: Unlocking more rooms, collecting more relics and cards. Clearing more missions to reach floor bosses until fully unlocking the library.



Themes: Fantasy, Adventure
